President Trump informed reporters on Sunday that God had spoken to him directly, at length, and in what he characterized as the warmest possible terms.

The conversation, which the President said took place on Saturday evening and lasted the better part of an hour, was initiated by the Almighty. Mr. Trump was emphatic on this point. He had not called God, he said. God had called him, and had been kept waiting only briefly.

According to the President, the Lord opened by remarking that He had been watching for some time and had never seen anything like it. Aides present for the announcement nodded.

> “He said, ‘Sir, nobody has done what you have done.’ He was almost in tears. A very big voice, by the way. Strong.”
>
> The President, describing the Creator

Asked what else had been discussed, the President said that God had raised several matters and that he, Mr. Trump, had been able to set Him straight on most of them. The two are understood to have left on excellent terms, with the President agreeing to take a second call if his schedule permits.

The White House declined to release a recording or transcript, describing the exchange as a private communication between two leaders. A spokesman added that the call had been perfect, and that anyone who had heard it, had anyone heard it, would say the same.

Theologians reached on Sunday night were cautious. A professor of divinity observed that Scripture records God speaking to a number of men, and that in each case the man is described as having done most of the listening. He could find no precedent for the reverse.

Members of the President’s party were quick to welcome the endorsement. Several said they had long suspected it. One senator, asked whether he believed the account, replied that he had no reason to doubt it and several reasons not to be asked again.

The President closed his remarks by saying that God had asked to be remembered to the American people, and had offered, without being prompted, to confirm the size of the crowd.

God could not be reached for comment.
