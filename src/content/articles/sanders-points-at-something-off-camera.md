Senator Bernie Sanders brought a press conference to a halt on Thursday by pointing, firmly and without explanation, at something beyond the cameras. His office later confirmed that the object indicated was the billionaire class.

The Senator had been answering a question about highway funding. Witnesses say he stopped in the middle of a sentence, raised his arm, and kept it raised. Several reporters turned around. They found a lawn, a hedge, and a gardener, who has since been cleared.

> “The Senator was pointing at the top one-tenth of one percent. He is always pointing at them. On this occasion his arm happened to be up.”
>
> A spokesman for the Senator

Aides explained that the billionaires are not in any one place and may therefore be pointed at in any direction, a fact they said the Senator has relied upon for half a century. A staff member is assigned to lower the arm at the end of each working day.

The Senator resumed his remarks after eleven minutes. He told reporters that he had said the same thing in 1974, in 1991, and on a Tuesday in 2009, and that he intended to go on saying it until someone wrote it down correctly.

Asked whether he had anything new to add, he said that he did not, that this was the point, and that the question was itself a distraction paid for by the people behind the hedge.

The billionaires issued no statement. Three of them are understood to have bought the hedge.
