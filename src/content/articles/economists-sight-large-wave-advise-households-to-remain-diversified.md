A panel of leading economists confirmed on Friday that a wave of considerable size is now approaching the household sector, and urged families in its path to stay calm, stay invested, and avoid making any decisions based on what they can see.

The wave, which the panel measured at several times the height of the boats beneath it, was first reported by the people in the boats. Their account was set aside at the time as anecdotal.

> “Nobody could have seen this coming. We say so with confidence, having been asked about it repeatedly while it came.”
>
> The chairman of the panel

The panel’s report runs to two hundred pages and concludes that the fundamentals remain sound. It observes that the sea level, averaged across the whole ocean, has scarcely changed, and that households who feel wet are experiencing a local condition.

Rowers are advised to consider their long-term goals. Those nearing retirement may wish to row somewhat faster. Those without a boat are reminded that boats have historically been an excellent investment and that the best time to buy one was thirty years ago.

A minority of the panel dissented, arguing that the wave is transitory and will pass through the economy within one to two quarters, taking with it only those things that were not fastened down, such as savings.

The mountain in the distance declined to comment, but was said by a spokesman to be well capitalized.
