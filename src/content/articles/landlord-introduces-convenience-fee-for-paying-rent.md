Residents of the Halcyon Arms were notified on Monday that the payment of rent will henceforth carry a Convenience Fee of thirty-five dollars a month, in recognition of the ease with which rent may now be paid.

The fee applies to payments made through the building’s online portal, which is the only means of payment the building accepts. The management company, Pinnacle Living Solutions, said in a notice slipped under each door that the portal represented a considerable investment and that it was only fair for those who benefit from it to share in the cost of being charged by it.

Tenants who would prefer not to pay the Convenience Fee may decline it by submitting a Convenience Waiver, for which there is a processing charge of forty dollars.

> “Our residents asked for flexibility. They may now choose between two fees.”
>
> A spokesman for Pinnacle Living Solutions

The new charge joins a schedule that already includes an Amenity Fee, a Common Area Fee, a Fee Administration Fee, and a monthly charge for Trash Valet, a service under which tenants carry their trash to the curb as before and are thanked for it by electronic mail.

A spokesman for the company rejected the suggestion that the fees amount to an increase in rent. Rent at the Halcyon Arms, he observed, has not risen in two years, and the company is proud of that record and advertises it on the side of the building.

He said the fees reflect the true cost of services that tenants had previously been receiving without knowing it. Among these he listed the hallway, the mailboxes, and the door.

One tenant, a schoolteacher who has lived in the building for nine years, said she had telephoned the management office to object. She was placed on hold for forty minutes and has since received a bill for it.

Pinnacle Living Solutions said it values its residents and welcomes their comments, which may be submitted through the portal.
