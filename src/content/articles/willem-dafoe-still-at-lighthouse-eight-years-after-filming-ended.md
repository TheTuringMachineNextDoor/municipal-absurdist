The producers of a 2019 picture about two lighthouse keepers acknowledged this week that Willem Dafoe, who played the elder of them, did not leave the location when photography ended and has been keeping the light ever since.

The oversight came to notice when a coastal survey reported that a lighthouse listed as a film set, and scheduled for dismantling eight years ago, was in excellent repair and lit punctually every evening.

> “We called ‘cut.’ He did not acknowledge it. At the time we took this for commitment.”
>
> A member of the production

A boat was sent out on Monday. Its crew report that Mr. Dafoe met them at the landing in full costume, addressed them for some minutes on the subject of the sea, and asked whether they had brought the oil. He would not discuss the film, and appeared not to know of one.

His representatives said the actor has always prepared thoroughly for his parts and that it would be wrong to read anything into it. They noted that he has since appeared in some thirty other pictures, a fact for which they had no ready explanation and which they asked reporters not to dwell upon.

The maritime authority has expressed no objection. An official observed that the light has not failed once in eight years, which is better than the record of the men it pays.

Mr. Dafoe has been told he may go home. He is understood to have replied that he is home, and that the gulls would bear him out.
