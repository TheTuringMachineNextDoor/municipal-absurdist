Sam Altman sought on Wednesday to calm public unease about his company’s newest artificial intelligence, telling an audience that the system posed no more danger to humanity than was required to remain competitive.

Mr. Altman, speaking softly and in a sweater, described the model as the most powerful technology ever built and said he lay awake at night in fear of it. He then announced that it would be available to the public on Thursday.

> “This may be the most important and most frightening thing our species has ever made. We are also offering forty percent off the annual plan.”
>
> Mr. Altman

Asked what the system could do, Mr. Altman said that no one fully understood it, least of all the people who made it, and that this was among its most exciting features. Early users report that it can write a sonnet, pass the bar examination, and state with complete assurance that there are two letters R in the word “strawberry.”

The company has taken precautions. A committee on safety was formed last year, and though it has since been dissolved, Mr. Altman said its spirit remains. A second committee has been asked to determine what the first one was worried about.

He closed by calling on governments to regulate the industry at once, firmly, and in a manner to be drafted by the industry.

Investors welcomed the remarks. The company is now valued at a sum which, an analyst noted, the model itself was unable to add up.
