# The Municipal Absurdist
*The public is advised to remain calm.*

## The Idea

The Municipal Absurdist is an independent digital satire publication covering the absurdities of modern life with the seriousness of a newspaper and the humor they deserve.

From world affairs and national politics to state government, local bureaucracy, education, money, culture, and everyday human behavior, the publication finds comedy in the contradictions of a world that takes itself far too seriously.

It is politically independent, skeptical of institutions and ideologies, and committed to intelligent, entertaining satire. Its purpose is to make people laugh, expose absurdities, and publish work that is funny, thoughtful, original, and worth sharing.

## The Main Sections

- **POLITICS:** World affairs, national politics, state government, local government, and bureaucracy.
- **CULTURE:** Entertainment, technology, internet culture, social trends, and everyday life.
- **EDUCATION:** Schools, universities, academia, and intellectual life.
- **MONEY:** Business, economics, work, consumerism, housing, and the cost of living.
- **COMIC:** Editorial cartoons, caricatures, comic strips, and visual satire.
- **GAMES:** Quizzes, puzzles, crosswords, trivia, and interactive humor.

The homepage features the lead satirical news story, recent articles, illustrations, recurring features, comics, games, and newsletter signup.

## The Visual Identity

The publication combines the credibility of an old newspaper with the visual curiosity of an illustrated literary journal. Its identity draws on historical newspaper mastheads, engraved illustrations, distinctive typography, and the editorial character of *The Public Domain Review*.

The site should feel authentic, collected, and slightly eccentric, while remaining clean, modern, readable, and easy to navigate.

## The Creative Community

The Municipal Absurdist welcomes creative work in many forms. It is a publication for writers, illustrators, cartoonists, poets, satirists, photographers, and other artists whose work fits its spirit.

**Write for Us:** Submit satirical articles, essays, humorous opinion pieces, short stories, poetry, scripts, reviews, and other written work.

**Draw for Us:** Submit editorial cartoons, caricatures, comic strips, illustrations, visual jokes, graphic art, and other artwork.

**Create for Us:** An open door for other formats, including photography, games, puzzles, interactive projects, and experimental creative work.

Contributors can pitch ideas, submit finished work, and build a portfolio through publication. Submission guidelines explain what the paper is looking for, how to submit, and how accepted work is credited and compensated where applicable.

## The Store

The Store is the publication's creative marketplace: a place to buy distinctive products, discover artists, commission original work, and support the people who make the paper.

It has three principal parts:

- **Merchandise:** Thoughtfully designed clothing, tote bags, notebooks, posters, pins, and other products featuring the publication's identity, original designs, and occasional special collections. The aim is smart, attractive merchandise that people would want to own, not generic promotional swag.

- **Art & Prints:** Original illustrations, editorial cartoons, limited-edition prints, posters, and artwork from the publication's contributors. Readers can purchase existing work, explore artists' portfolios, and inquire about commissioned illustrations.

- **Commissions:** A way to hire participating artists and writers for custom work. This could include caricatures, illustrations, portraits, poems, short stories, speeches, and other personalized creative pieces. Each commission has its own terms, pricing or quote process, and delivery expectations.

The Store gives contributors a potential way to earn money from their work while allowing readers to support independent art and journalism. It should feel like a small, curated independent art shop attached to a publication, rather than a conventional corporate merchandise store.

## About, Readers, and Support

**About:** The publication's mission, founder, staff, contributors, history, and contact information.

**Reader Features:** Accounts, likes, comments, sharing, and saved articles, supported by appropriate moderation.

**Newsletter:** A regular email featuring the best new articles, illustrations, comics, and other work.

**Support:** Donations to help sustain the publication, with the possibility of memberships or additional supporter benefits as it grows.

## The Guiding Principle

**A real newspaper for an absurd world, and a home for the people who find it funny.**

The Municipal Absurdist should be more than a website full of jokes. It should become a distinctive publication with a recognizable voice, a rich visual identity, a community of creative contributors, and a marketplace that helps those contributors share and sell their work.

It should take its craft seriously without ever taking itself too seriously.
